Contact

One maintainer. Issues are the fastest route.

Bugs, build failures, and documentation defects

Open an issue at github.com/deraldg/x64base/issues. A build that fails while following the published instructions counts as a documentation defect and is worth reporting as one.

Email

No GitHub account, or something that does not fit an issue? Write to deraldg@gmail.com. Issues are still preferred for anything another user might benefit from finding later.

Teaching use

If you want to use x64base in a course, say so in an issue. The teaching material is the part most likely to be shaped by someone actually trying to teach with it, and it is currently shaped by one person's guess about that.

Contributing

The project is one person's work and the internal structure moves quickly. Small, well-scoped patches and evidence — a regression that fails, a transcript that contradicts a claim on this site — are more useful right now than large features.

The most useful thing you can send. A capability listed here as runtime-proven that does not reproduce on your machine. That is the claim the whole site rests on, and finding a hole in it is a favor.
